Read before using these numbers. All four models are descriptive, not causal. They are trained on FAO's published series - the undernourishment target is itself a modelled estimate - and validated with strictly time-based splits (train on earlier years, test on later ones); metrics below are from the held-out years. The risk score is a screening aid for where to look first, not a basis for allocating resources without reading the country context. Source archives and load statistics

Every domain is a FAOSTAT bulk archive, hashed on download. Rows are dropped only for missing-value flags, out-of-scope elements/years, or exact duplicate keys.

Provenance of loaded observations

FAOSTAT flags: A = official, E = estimated, I = imputed, X = external organisation.

Data-quality gate

Checks run on every build and stored in dq_check. A failure aborts the pipeline.

Schema and conventions

  • One long fact table observation(domain, area_code, item_code, element_code, year) with conformed dim_area / dim_item / dim_element / dim_flag / dim_domain.
  • FAOSTAT codes are kept verbatim; item codes are text because the food-security domain uses suffixed codes (e.g. 210091F).
  • 3-year averages such as "2020-2022" are stored at the middle year (2021) with the original label retained.
  • Regional aggregates (area codes ≥ 5000, plus "China" 351 and "Sub-Saharan Africa" 420) are flagged is_aggregate and never mixed with countries.
  • Countries are enriched with UN M49 region / sub-region / LDC / LLDC / SIDS via their M49 code.
  • Known source quirk: FS indicator 21034 "arable land equipped for irrigation" is effectively expressed against total land area, so irrigation shares are rebuilt from the land-use domain.
  • Population projections (to 2100) and emission scenarios (2030/2050) are excluded by the year window.
